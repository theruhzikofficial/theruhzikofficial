About The Ruhzik • Our Story

The Sound, The People, The Beginning.

Formed in New Delhi in 2026, The Ruhzik brings together four musicians who share a love for melody, rhythm and live performance. Our sound draws on Bollywood and indie music, with a touch of rock and a rhythm-driven approach.

We reinterpret familiar songs through our own arrangements, medleys and mashups, creating room for audience interaction and spontaneous moments on stage. Alongside our live covers, we are developing original music. The members have performed individually at numerous events, and The Ruhzik is now performance-ready for its debut public performances.
